Privacy

Your designs are yours.

Plain-language version, no legal wall of text. Last updated August 2026.

What do you collect?

Just your design file — sent only when you click "Open in StitchBase" in Inkscape. We use it to generate your preview, preflight findings, and chat. That's it.

Do you use it for anything else?

No. Your design isn't sold, shared, or used to market to you. There's no account and no personal info required to use StitchBase.

How long do you keep it?

Your review session is anonymous and auto-deletes after 7 days. No login, nothing to manage — it just clears itself out.

This is a beta — what does that mean for privacy?

We're a small team still building StitchBase, so treat this as our early, honest-effort policy rather than a lawyer-grade one. As we grow we'll expand this page with more detail.

Questions? Email stitchbaseai@gmail.com.
